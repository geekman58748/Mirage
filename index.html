Under the Hood

How Mirage Works

Four simple steps to shield your business revenue from public view.

1
Create Payment Link
Merchant generates a checkout link via Mirage API, SDK, or dashboard for a specific item or invoice.
2
Generate Ephemeral Addr
MagicBlock instantiates a brand new, one-time destination address for the customer's checkout session.
3
Standard Checkout
Customer pays normally using Phantom, Backpack, or Solflare. Total transaction time remains sub-second.
4
Private Settlement
Funds are swept directly to the merchant's private treasury without linking customer history on-chain.
